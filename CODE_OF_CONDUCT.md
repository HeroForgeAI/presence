# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and every other project space.

## The short version

- Be kind and patient. Many people who use this app are not programmers, and no question about it is too basic.
- Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly.
- Critique code and ideas, never the person.
- No harassment, no insults, no sexual language or imagery, and no publishing of anyone's private information.
- Never post a real take, a screenshot of a real meeting, or someone else's face.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here breaks these rules, tell the maintainer privately. Open a private report at <https://github.com/HeroForgeAI/presence/security/advisories/new> and begin the title with "Conduct". Only the maintainer can read it.

Every report is read and handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that break these rules, and may block a person who keeps breaking them. The enforcement steps in the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) are the guide.
